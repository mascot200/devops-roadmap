## LinkedIn

Most DevOps advice is a list of tools. This is a path.

I published a free, open-source DevOps roadmap that takes you from your first Linux command to senior-level architecture, in the order that makes sense.

What is inside:
- 21 levels: Linux, networking, Git, Docker, CI/CD, AWS, Terraform, Kubernetes, observability, security, SRE, GitOps and more
- 248 topics, each with a real-world example, diagrams, a step-by-step guide and hands-on tasks
- 1,700+ tasks with a way to check each one, so you know when you actually got it
- 9 projects that grow into one production-style system

The goal is not to know 50 tools. It is to take one app to production and make it secure, observable, scalable, highly available, automatically deployable, recoverable and cost-efficient.

Plan for 8 to 12 months at 2 to 3 hours a day to reach a strong practical level. Senior judgment takes longer, and it comes from real production work.

It is MIT licensed. Fork it, use it with your team, send fixes.

Roadmap: github.com/mascot200/devops-roadmap
